# Cascade 1.4.0

Status: source release and local image candidate. I have not published `miladsade96/cascade:1.4.0` to Docker Hub.

This release completes the coordinator architecture milestone: certified majority decisions, cross-node resolution of in-doubt work, stale-owner reconciliation, bounded per-shard journals, atomic checkpoint replacement, separate group and delivery mutation monitors, shard-specific readiness, recovery metrics, and a pinned format-11-to-format-12 source campaign.

## Qualification

- The 1,000-resident-group campaign acknowledged 3,000/3,000 writes, recovered 1,000/1,000 final offsets, used all three owners, and passed controller loss plus full restart with 668/668 quorum transactions and no failure or admission rejection.
- The pinned format-11 rolling campaign completed ten phases, accepted rollback before activation, rejected downgrade after activation, preserved majority traffic, restored the removed broker, and consumed exactly 40/40 records.
- The final Scala unit, integration, end-to-end, fault, security, and qualification run passed **539/539** tests with no failures or skips in **153 seconds** on Eclipse Adoptium Java 21.0.11.
- The release regression includes a three-broker Kafka transaction that stages a group offset, loses its coordinator, commits through the surviving quorum, and recovers both the record and exact offset. The final fix preserves the combined group-plus-transaction checkpoint through the delivery callback adapter.

The detailed measurements and machine-readable reports are in the [coordinator completion qualification](../performance/2026-09-12-coordinator-completion.md).
